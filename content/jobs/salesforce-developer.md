## About the role

Our client, a [[mid-sized product company]], is expanding its Salesforce team. You'll work with sales and service operations to build and improve how they use the platform.

## What you'll do

- Build custom objects, fields, formulas, validation rules, flows and approval processes
- Develop Apex classes, triggers and Lightning Web Components
- Migrate and clean data using Data Loader and related tools
- Configure Salesforce to match business processes for leads, accounts, opportunities, products and forecasting
- Work closely with admins, business analysts and QA

## What you'll need

- 3+ years of hands-on Salesforce development
- Strong Apex, SOQL and Lightning (LWC or Aura)
- Good understanding of standard Sales and Service Cloud objects and processes
- A background in Java or another object-oriented language
- Someone who enjoys working in a team

## Nice to have

- Platform Developer I or II certification
- Integration experience (REST/SOAP APIs)
